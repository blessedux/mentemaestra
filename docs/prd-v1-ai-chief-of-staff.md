# Product Requirements Document
## Mente Maestra V1
### AI Chief of Staff

Status: Draft  
Owner: Mente Maestra

---

# Problem

Founder-led service businesses spend enormous effort operating disconnected tools.

CRM.

Email.

Notes.

Meetings.

Documents.

Lead generation.

Websites.

Strategy.

None of them share context.

Founders become the integration layer.

This creates cognitive overload.

---

# Vision

Create an AI Chief of Staff that continuously learns the business and executes high leverage operational missions.

V1 focuses on Lead Generation.

---

# Success Criteria

Within 30 days customers should have:

- Defined ICP
- Qualified prospects
- Active outbound campaigns
- AI-assisted follow-up
- Sales conversations
- Booked meetings

---

# Target User

## Founder

Age: 40–60

## Business

- Traditional service companies
- 5–30 employees
- Non-technical founders
- Already profitable
- Values simplicity over customization

---

# Goals

- Reduce operational complexity
- Reduce founder workload
- Increase qualified sales conversations
- Create reusable business knowledge
- Build long-term memory

---

# Non Goals

- Voice agents
- Phone calling
- Complex ERP
- Accounting
- Multi-agent orchestration UI
- Autonomous contract negotiation
